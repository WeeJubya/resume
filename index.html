DESIGN · DEVELOP · DEPLOY

Case Study · Programme Design

Analyst Developer Programme (ADP)

The Analyst Development Programme was created to deliver a consistent, structured training experience for all new analysts, ensuring a shared foundation of knowledge and skills. It was a collaborative initiative between the in-house development team and an external training provider, combining subject matter expertise with tailored learning design.

Audience
All new analysts
Tools
Articulate Storyline, Adobe Creative Suite
Delivery
Agile design, build and review cycles
Impact
More than 100 cohorts of 12–16 analysts by 2024
01

Planning & Scoping

Engaged key stakeholders to define learning objectives, target audience needs and delivery constraints; developed the curriculum map and detailed project plan.

02

Design & Prototyping

Built storyboards for priority modules, piloted a prototype and integrated early feedback into design templates for wider use.

03

Full Development

Developed modules in agile sprints covering scripting, asset creation, build, peer review and stakeholder sign-off, with shared version-controlled tracking.

04

Review & Deployment

Conducted accessibility checks, browser/device testing and stakeholder walkthroughs, then packaged modules to SCORM for phased LMS/LXP releases.

Impact & reflection

As of 2024, more than 100 cohorts of between 12–16 analysts had completed the programme.

Delivering the ADP highlighted the value of collaboration in achieving a consistent and high-quality learner experience. Early alignment on learning outcomes and standards helped both teams develop coherent, accessible and relevant content, while feedback from early cohorts directly informed improvements in content sequencing and delivery methods.
